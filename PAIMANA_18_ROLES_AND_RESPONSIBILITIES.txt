PAIMANA PREDICT — 18 INSTITUTIONAL ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES SPECIFICATION
AI-Powered Infrastructure Risk Intelligence & Early Warning System
Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India
Problem Statement 26103 (Smart India Hackathon 2026)

OVERVIEW
--------
The operational governance architecture of PAIMANA PREDICT is structured into 
18 specialized institutional personas across 4 governance tiers. Each role is 
strictly scoped with its own authorized workspace, operational responsibilities, 
and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) boundaries to eliminate data leakage.

================================================================================
TIER 1: EXECUTIVE & CENTRAL MONITORING (MACRO OVERSIGHT)
================================================================================

1. SENIOR REVIEW & DECISION AUTHORITY
   - Role Key: senior_decision_maker (Alias: secretary, decision_maker)
   - Organization: Cabinet Secretariat / Prime Minister's Office (PMO)
   - Department: Cabinet Secretariat Infrastructure Cell
   - Default Workspace / Route: /risk-intelligence (Executive Decisions & Directives)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Monitors national macro capital exposure across the ₹42.78 Lakh Cr portfolio (1,981 projects).
     * Reviews critical project rankings, sector benchmarks, and systemic inter-ministerial disputes.
     * Evaluates projected cost escalation envelopes and delay distributions across 22 infrastructure sectors.
     * Records and issues high-level binding Cabinet Directives with assigned accountability.
     * Tracks the real-world resolution velocity and effectiveness of executive interventions.
     * Generates executive decision briefs for the Empowered Group of Secretaries (EGoS).

2. IPMD MONITORING & SURVEILLANCE OFFICER
   - Role Key: monitoring_officer (Alias: officer)
   - Organization: Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)
   - Department: Infrastructure and Project Monitoring Division (IPMD)
   - Default Workspace / Route: /overview (Portfolio Surveillance & Early Warning Signals)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Conducts daily continuous surveillance over all 1,981 central sector projects (costing >= ₹150 Cr).
     * Triages automated early warnings, weak deterioration signals, and velocity drop anomalies.
     * Opens governed monitoring cases and assigns time-bound interventions with strict SLA escalation timers.
     * Executes human-in-the-loop risk reviews with mandatory cryptographically logged justifications.
     * Tracks intervention recovery trajectories and escalates unresolved bottlenecks to line ministries.

3. ADMINISTRATIVE MINISTRY / PROJECT REVIEW OFFICER
   - Role Key: admin_ministry_review (Alias: ministry_reviewer)
   - Organization: Line Ministries (e.g., MoRTH, Ministry of Railways, Ministry of Power, DoT)
   - Department: Ministry Project Coordination Directorate
   - Default Workspace / Route: /ministry-overview (Line Ministry Review Hub)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Reviews ministry-scoped project portfolio, expenditure ceilings, and physical timelines.
     * Manages ministry-level bottleneck cases escalated from IPMD surveillance.
     * Issues corrective action directives to subordinate Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) and project authorities.
     * Monitors agency compliance responses and validates submitted ground evidence.
     * Escalates unresolved cross-departmental hurdles to IPMD and the Cabinet Secretariat.

================================================================================
TIER 2: IMPLEMENTING AGENCY & PROJECT EXECUTION (GROUND TRUTH)
================================================================================

4. PROJECT / NODAL OFFICER
   - Role Key: project_admin (Alias: nodal)
   - Organization: Project Implementing Agencies / PSUs (e.g., BBNL, NHAI PIU, RVNL)
   - Department: Project Execution Directorate
   - Default Workspace / Route: /projects/PAI-706775 (Assigned Project Hub)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Submits authoritative monthly physical progress actuals and verified cumulative expenditures.
     * Updates scheduled milestone deadlines, revised completion targets, and official delay causes.
     * Uploads ground evidence, engineering drawings, and geo-tagged site photographs.
     * Formulates formal action plans in response to IPMD early warning flags and monitoring cases.
     * Verifies contractor milestone completion claims before payment certification.

5. PROJECT ENGINEERING OFFICER
   - Role Key: project_engineering (Alias: engineer)
   - Organization: Technical & Engineering Wing (e.g., NHAI Engineering Division)
   - Department: Technical & Engineering Directorate
   - Default Workspace / Route: /engineering (Engineering Review Hub)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Conducts structural and technical progress assessments against approved Bill of Quantities (BoQ).
     * Evaluates site engineering hindrances, geological variations, and technical design changes.
     * Reviews milestone technical completions alongside Third Party Inspection (TPI) consultants.
     * Records site engineering observations and technical recommendations for rework or readiness.
     * Validates structural telemetry, drone imagery, and material testing data.

6. QUALITY & INSPECTION OFFICER
   - Role Key: quality_auditor (Alias: quality, auditor)
   - Organization: Third Party Inspection (TPI) Agencies (e.g., Engineers India Limited - EIL, RITES)
   - Department: TPI Inspection & Quality Assurance Directorate
   - Default Workspace / Route: /quality (Quality & Compliance Center)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Performs independent technical quality audits across active project construction packages.
     * Issues Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs) and manages their 6-stage verification lifecycle.
     * Inspects certified laboratory test reports (IS 516 concrete, IS 1786 rebar, IS 2720 soil).
     * Audits contractor rework submissions and signs independent TPI verification certificates.
     * Closes verified NCRs only after independent engineering re-tests (contractors cannot close their own NCRs).

7. PROJECT FINANCE & ACCOUNTS OFFICER
   - Role Key: project_finance (Alias: finance, financial_officer)
   - Organization: Project Finance & Accounts Division / Integrated Finance Division (IFD)
   - Department: Finance & Accounts Division
   - Default Workspace / Route: /finance (Project Financial Ledger)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Audits monthly cumulative expenditure claims against original sanctioned cost ceilings.
     * Detects physical-financial divergence (capital burn without proportional physical progress).
     * Reviews contractor payment claims, price variation clauses, and bank guarantee validity.
     * Prepares Revised Cost Estimate (RCE) financial dossiers and cost variance breakdowns.
     * Submits financial observations on cost overrun triggers and contingent liabilities.

8. CONTRACTOR / EPC REPRESENTATIVE
   - Role Key: contractor_rep (Alias: contractor, epc)
   - Organization: EPC Contractors / Concessionaires (e.g., L&T Infrastructure, BharatNet EPC Consortium)
   - Department: EPC Execution Consortium
   - Default Workspace / Route: /monthly-updates (Contractor Execution Hub)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Submits contract package physical milestone claims and daily work actuals.
     * Responds to Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs) and executes mandatory site rework.
     * Uploads NABL-certified laboratory test reports and material compliance records.
     * Submits geo-tagged ground progress evidence and drone visual telemetry.
     * Formulates formal hindrance claims regarding land handover, right-of-way, and utility shifting.

9. SUPERVISION CONSULTANT / PMC
   - Role Key: supervision_consultant (Alias: pmc_consultant, pmc)
   - Organization: Independent Project Management Consultancy (e.g., Tata Consulting Engineers)
   - Department: Independent Engineering & Supervision Cell
   - Default Workspace / Route: /supervision (PMC Supervision Workspace)
   - Core Responsibilities & Works:
     * Performs contractually mandated daily construction supervision and progress verification.
     * Conducts independent engineering measurements of completed contract milestones.
     * Submits independent technical observations and PMC recommendations to government authorities.
     * Reviews contractor quality test logs, safety protocols, and daily labor turnout records.
     * Escalates field hindrances, site safety breaches, and design deviations to implementing agencies.

TIER 3: COORDINATION & HIGHER REVIEW (CROSS-SECTOR)
================================================================================

10. INTER-MINISTERIAL COORDINATION OFFICER
    - Role Key: inter_ministerial_coordination (Alias: inter_coord)
    - Organization: Cabinet Secretariat / Coordination Wing
    - Department: Inter-Ministerial Project Group
    - Default Workspace / Route: /coordination (Inter-Ministerial Coordination Hub)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Coordinates multi-agency infrastructure conflicts where execution spans multiple ministries.
      * Manages cross-departmental bottleneck cases with defined agency ownership matrices.
      * Records minutes, binding commitments, and action items of Inter-Ministerial Committee (IMC) meetings.
      * Tracks statutory environmental, forest, railway crossing, and defense clearances.
      * Escalates chronic inter-agency deadlocks directly to the Cabinet Secretary / PMO.

11. STATE / CENTRAL PROJECT COORDINATION OFFICER
    - Role Key: state_coordination (Alias: state_coord)
    - Organization: State Infrastructure Coordination Cells (State Chief Secretary / PWD)
    - Department: State Urban Development & RoW Cell
    - Default Workspace / Route: /state-coordination (State Coordination Directorate)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Coordinates state-level dependencies: Land Acquisition awards, RoW handovers, and district clearances.
      * Manages utility shifting pipelines (high-voltage power transmission lines, water mains, gas pipelines).
      * Assigns bottleneck accountability to District Collectors and state utility boards.
      * Monitors state compliance deadlines and progress response packages.
      * Bridges field project authorities with state chief secretaries and central ministries.

12. GATISHAKTI / INFRASTRUCTURE NETWORK COORDINATOR
    - Role Key: gatishakti_officer (Alias: gatishakti)
    - Organization: DPIIT / PM GatiShakti National Master Plan
    - Department: Network Planning Group (NPG)
    - Default Workspace / Route: /risk-network (PM GatiShakti Network Topology)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Surveils multi-modal infrastructure network topology and shared Right-of-Way alignments.
      * Simulates cascading delay ripples: models how delay in an upstream project impacts downstream corridors.
      * Identifies topological bottleneck centrality across 1,981 central infrastructure assets.
      * Issues Network Planning Group (NPG) synchronization directives to line agencies.
      * Calculates downstream delay impact and secondary capital exposure across multi-modal logistics hubs.

13. INVESTMENT APPRAISAL & PROJECT REVIEW OFFICER
    - Role Key: investment_appraisal_reviewer (Alias: appraisal_officer)
    - Organization: Public Investment Board (PIB) / Expenditure Finance Committee (EFC)
    - Department: Department of Expenditure / PIB Secretariat
    - Default Workspace / Route: /investment-review (Investment Appraisal Directorate)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Evaluates pre-investment appraisal packages and Revised Cost Estimates (RCE).
      * Benchmarks proposed project costs against 22 historical infrastructure sectors.
      * Executes sensitivity simulations on Interest During Construction (IDC) and inflation impact.
      * Assembles comprehensive risk-adjusted project appraisal dossiers for appraisal bodies.
      * Reviews historical peer project performance to inform capital allocation decisions.

14. FINANCIAL REVIEW AUTHORITY
    - Role Key: financial_review_authority (Alias: fin_authority)
    - Organization: Integrated Finance Division (IFD), Ministry of Finance / DEA
    - Department: Capital Budget & Outlay Directorate
    - Default Workspace / Route: /financial-review (Fiscal Scrutiny Authority)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Scrutinizes macro fiscal risk exposure across the national ₹42.78 Lakh Cr capital envelope.
      * Evaluates mega-project Revised Cost Estimates (RCE) and macroeconomic cost escalation drivers.
      * Detects sectoral capital misallocation and long-tail cost divergence trends.
      * Recommends fiscal restructuring milestones and expenditure phasing ceilings.
      * Assesses sovereign contingent liabilities arising from prolonged project disputes and arbitration.

15. INDEPENDENT AUDIT / COMPLIANCE OBSERVER
    - Role Key: audit_observer (Alias: cag)
    - Organization: Office of the Comptroller & Auditor General (CAG) of India
    - Department: Independent Project Audit & Compliance Cell
    - Default Workspace / Route: /audit (Statutory Audit Vault)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Conducts independent, strictly read-only examination of the append-only cryptographic audit trail.
      * Inspects historical monthly project snapshots, baseline revisions, and financial sanction history.
      * Reviews human risk-override justifications to ensure transparency and prevent arbitrary manipulation.
      * Traces end-to-end data lineage from source flash reports to predictive ML risk outputs.
      * Exports statutory compliance audit dossiers for legislative scrutiny.

TIER 4: PREDICTIVE INTELLIGENCE & PLATFORM GOVERNANCE (AI & SECURITY)
================================================================================

16. PREDICTIVE RISK & DATA ANALYST
    - Role Key: risk_analyst (Alias: analyst, data_analyst)
    - Organization: NITI Aayog Data Analytics Unit / MoSPI Analytics Cell
    - Department: Infrastructure Intelligence Directorate
    - Default Workspace / Route: /predictions (Predictive Intelligence Hub)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Inspects and fine-tunes temporal Gradient Boosting models (time-gbm-v1.4: ROC-AUC 0.8850, Brier 0.1714).
      * Analyzes 90-day delay probabilities and cost overrun risk scores across all 1,981 projects.
      * Evaluates multi-period backtesting calibration curves (ECE 0.041) and Brier scores.
      * Inspects SHAP feature importances, anomaly scores, and risk momentum distributions.
      * Conducts scenario stress testing (monsoon intensity, supply chain price shocks, labor availability).

17. AI GOVERNANCE & MODEL ASSURANCE OFFICER
    - Role Key: ai_governance (Alias: aigov)
    - Organization: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
    - Department: National Model Assurance & Ethics Board
    - Default Workspace / Route: /model-governance (AI Assurance & Rule T Gatekeeper)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Enforces Rule T temporal anti-leakage compliance (strictly t <= T for all feature calculations).
      * Audits model cards, algorithmic fairness, training lineage, and evaluation metrics.
      * Monitors Kolmogorov-Smirnov feature drift and Population Stability Index (PSI).
      * Gatekeeps and signs off on production model promotions.
      * Evaluates calibration reliability and decision-threshold trade-offs under high-risk thresholds.

18. DATA, PLATFORM & SECURITY ADMINISTRATOR
    - Role Key: data_platform_security_admin (Alias: sysadmin, system_admin)
    - Organization: MoSPI / National Platform Architecture Cell
    - Department: Platform Infrastructure & Security Operations Center (SOC)
    - Default Workspace / Route: /settings (Platform Administration & SOC)
    - Core Responsibilities & Works:
      * Manages user identity provisioning, 18-persona RBAC access controls, and cryptographic JWT sessions.
      * Oversees Table 6 project data ingestion and verifies 100.0% mathematical data reconciliation.
      * Audits SOC telemetry, brute-force login attempts, 403 access violations, and background worker queues.
      * Inspects system health endpoints (/health, /health/data, /health/ml).
      * Enforces security governance policies, cryptographic audit logging integrity, and system configurations.
