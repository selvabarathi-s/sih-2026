## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This gap analysis reviews the entire **PAIMANA Predict** codebase against the requirements of Smart India Hackathon 2026 Problem Statement **SIH26103** and the 52-part advanced architecture specification.

Every functional area has been audited across:
1. **Existing Feature**: What is currently present in the codebase.
2. **Current Status**: Implemented, Partial / Simulated, or Missing.
3. **Missing Capability**: The operational or government decision-support requirement that remains unmet.
4. **Technical Gap**: Architectural, mathematical, API, or data model deficiencies.
5. **Priority**: 
   - **P0**: Critical for SIH Demonstration (Must Have)
   - **P1**: High-Value Architecture & Operational Depth
   - **P2**: Optional / Future Horizon (Deferred until P0/P1 are stable)
6. **Dependencies**: Upstream services, models, or data structures needed.
7. **Recommended Implementation**: Precise design pattern and module.
8. **Risk of Implementation**: Potential regression or methodological hazard.

## 3. Prioritized Implementation Roadmap

Based on the audit matrix, implementation is executed in strict sequence:

### Phase 1 — Core P0 Risk & Prediction Foundation
1. **Dynamic Risk Momentum & Transitions** (`riskScoreEngine.js`, `inferenceService.js`).
2. **Confidence & Uncertainty Engine** (`confidenceEngine.js`).
3. **As-Of Prediction Engine & Route** (`asOfService.js`, `/as-of-prediction`, `AsOfPredictionPage.tsx`).
4. **Project Criticality & Intervention Priority Score** (`priorityEngine.js`).
5. **Alert Deduplication & Composite Early Warnings** (`compositeAlertEngine.js`, `EarlyWarningsPage.tsx`).

### Phase 2 — Governance, Security & Provenance (P0)
6. **Granular Provenance Badges** across all cards and detail tabs.
7. **Human-in-the-Loop Override Engine** with immutable audit logging.
8. **Resource-Level RBAC Hardening** across project updates and TopNav role switcher.
9. **Reusable Standardized Project Health Card** (`ProjectHealthCard.tsx`).
10. **Interactive Project Timeline with Risk Overlay** (`ProjectTimeline.tsx`).

### Phase 3 — Prescriptive Depth & Closed-Loop Intelligence (P1)
11. **Intervention Scenario Simulator (What-If)** (`scenarioService.js`, `ScenarioModal.tsx`).
12. **Intervention Effectiveness & Recovery Learning** (`interventionEffectivenessService.js`).
13. **Project Similarity / Historical Twin Finder** (`projectSimilarityService.js`).
14. **Financial Exposure Portfolio Waterfall** (`portfolioService.js`).
15. **Platform Resilience & Fragility Indices** (`resilienceService.js`).
16. **Quality & Compliance Evidence Tab** (`qualityService.js`).
17. **Model Agreement / Disagreement Consensus Meter** (`PredictionsPage.tsx`).

### Phase 4 — Demonstration Acceleration & Verification (P0)
18. **Judge Demo Mode Controller** (`JudgeDemoController.tsx` with 12-step guided flow).
19. **Comprehensive Verification Test Suite** (`tests/verify_p0_suite.py`).
20. **Final Documentation & Feature Matrix** (`docs/final-feature-matrix.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
